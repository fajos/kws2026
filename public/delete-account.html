Data Safety & Privacy Compliance

FRA Journal — Account & Data Deletion Request

In accordance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) and Google Play Data Safety policies, you have full authority to request the permanent deletion of your FRA Journal account and all associated cloud data.

What Data is Deleted

  • Account Profile & Credentials: Full name, registered email address, password hash, and salt.
  • Personal Journal Data: Yearly targets, quarterly milestones, monthly reflections, weekly actions, daily logs, habits, and radar balance data.
  • Cloud Backups: All encrypted backup synchronization archives stored on our servers.
  • Reminders & Schedules: Automated check-in emails and cadence notifications.

What Data is Retained

None. All personal journal content, goal records, and user identifiers are permanently purged from our database once deletion is processed.

Subscription Notice: If you subscribed to FRA Journal Pro through Apple App Store or Google Play, remember to cancel the subscription in your device's Apple ID or Google Play store settings to prevent automatic renewal by Apple or Google.

Deletion Timeline

  • In-App Self-Service: Instantaneous and permanent.
  • Web Form with Verified Password: Instantaneous.
  • Web Request / Email Support: Processed within 24 to 48 hours.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the FRA Journal mobile app installed, you can wipe your account immediately:

  1. Open the FRA Journal app on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings gear icon at the top.
  3. Scroll to the Cloud Backup & Account section.
  4. Tap Delete Account & Wipe Cloud Data and confirm.
