Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026 • FRA Journal

1. Overview & Commitment

FRA Journal (“the App”), developed by Finish Rich Africa (FRA) and Fajostech, is designed to help you organize your yearly goals, milestones, reflections, and personal habits. We believe your thoughts, finances, and reflections are deeply personal. We do not sell your personal data or journal entries to any third party.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register an account, we collect your name, email address, and a salted cryptographic password hash to synchronize your journal data securely across devices.
  • Journal Content: Your yearly targets, quarterly milestones, monthly reflections, and weekly plans are stored locally on your device and, if signed in, backed up securely to our encrypted cloud database.
  • Biometric Data: If you enable Face ID, Touch ID, or Fingerprint unlock, authentication is processed exclusively on-device by your operating system’s native security hardware (Apple Secure Enclave / Android BiometricPrompt). We never collect, transmit, or store your biometric data.

3. Subscriptions & Payments

All in-app purchases made on iOS or Android are processed directly through Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment card numbers. We receive anonymous transaction identifiers from RevenueCat solely to verify your Pro entitlement across iOS, Android, and Web.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored locally on your device via secure sandbox storage and backed up via encrypted HTTPS connections to our secure database infrastructure. All database communication uses industry-standard TLS encryption.

5. User Control & Account Deletion

In accordance with Apple App Store and Google Play guidelines, you have full control over your data. You may permanently delete your account and all associated cloud entries at any time directly in the app under Settings > Delete Account & Wipe Cloud Data.
